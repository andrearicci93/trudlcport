# Tomb Raider: Underworld - game knowledge base

What the project learned about the game itself (PC 1.1, Xbox 360 retail and title update, DLC 1 *Beneath the Ashes*, DLC 2 *Lara's Shadow*) from its data and code, written at game level: what happens, under which conditions, with which numbers.
Status: ✅ proven from data / code or seen live · 🔶 inferred · ⬜ open. Where a later finding corrected an earlier one only the corrected fact is listed, marked "(corrected)".
Technical detail (addresses, structs, tools) is in [ENGINE.md](ENGINE.md); the Source column names its section and row, a repo doc, or a `Befund-*` finding report (kept outside this repo, named only).

|---|---|---|---|

| Scale | About 290 units per metre (280-310; Lara's bind mesh is 511 units tall) (corrected: not 100 per metre) | 🔶 | ENGINE §4.3 |

| Fall damage | Only a landing move that runs the fall check hurts Lara, once per landing. The height counts from where she left the ground (the take-off, not the top of the jump) down to where she lands. Base-game Lara: up to 4.4 m nothing, 4.4 to 6.3 m up to half of her maximum health, 6.3 to 7.6 m from half to all, above 7.6 m all of it - dead on every difficulty, because the damage is a share of the maximum health and the difficulty factor is not applied. DLC 2 Lara: 8.7 / 10.9 / 13.1 m. Some landing moves scale it (0.6 / 0.3) or check up to 4 s later; landings into moves without the check (catching a pole or ledge, water) take none 🔶. | Jump flight | A jump in the air is a real parabola: Lara gets a launch velocity and falls with a fixed gravity of about 5150 units/s² (same for every Lara). When the game has picked a jump target (a pole, ledge or other grab point) it computes the velocity so that the parabola meets that point after the planned flight time, and it re-aims every frame, so Lara homes in on the target. Once she has fallen further than the deadly height, the target is dropped - no catching help any more. Where the velocity of a jump without a target comes from is still open 🔶 | ✅ static | ENGINE §12 "Lara jump: launch, gravity, aimed parabola (R2b)" |
| Jump kinds | (Corrected by R11, next row: the jump speeds are in Lara's jump table.) A jump without a target gets only the speed of the thing she stood on at that one call (zero on fixed ground); the real launch speed comes from the jump table. A jump with a target first simulates four steps of the take-off animation, takes the flight time from that and then aims at the target. Letting go of the grapple rope throws her off with the rope's swing speed, so it depends on the swing phase. Thrown objects fall with their own, weaker gravity (about 2700 units/s²) and bounce and slow down by the physics rules; the game has grenades (base game and both DLCs), but how a grenade or a carried weight is thrown is still open 🔶 | ✅ static | ENGINE §12 "Launch sites by kind (R10)", "Thrown objects and the physics body (R10)" |
| Jump preview and catch reach | Lara carries a table of 106 jump kinds; each gives a launch speed (forward and up, turned with her facing: a forward jump 74.3 forward and 42.5 up per frame, a jump straight up 48.0 up, a jump onto a target 40 forward and 50 up), how far ahead the arc is followed (40 frames by default) and limits for angle and distance. The jump target itself is picked only at the take-off (live), by following that arc and collecting the grab points (poles, ledges) whose boxes touch it. With a target the upward speed stays as in the table and the game takes the flight time at which the arc reaches the target's height (chapel pole: 25.6 frames = exactly the 790-unit drop), then sets the forward speed so she arrives there and corrects it every frame while the time counts down. So a dashed preview can use the same numbers but must run the search itself. Whether she catches a pole she jumps past depends on whether the pole's grab line is still found along this arc within the kind's limits (angle limit 90 degrees, distance limits 300 units are candidates 🔶) - not on a fixed catch radius; after take-off the arc is re-aimed at the chosen point, until she has fallen past the deadly height. | Which jump a press makes (20.11.) | The jump kind is not chosen while Lara stands: each move of her chart can name a kind, and the value seen before a press is just the kind of the last move entered (on the balance beam a small step-down kind of the beam family).
